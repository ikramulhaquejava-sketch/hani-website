The Heritage Statement

A Legacy Forged in Moradabad.

For centuries, the city of Moradabad has been synonymous with the mastery of metal. Our factory and showroom, situated on the historic Baradari Road, harness this generational lineage.

We combine old-world artisanship with contemporary design sensibilities, delivering bespoke metal solutions that meet the rigorous demands of elite interior designers and luxury retail chains across the Americas, Europe, and the Middle East.
